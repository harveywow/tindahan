# User Guide — for Aling Nena

This guide walks through one store day. Switch between **EN** and **FIL** with the button at the top right. Screens are named here in Filipino with the English name in brackets, e.g. **Paninda (Products)**.

## Before the first day

1. Go to **Ayos (Settings)**.
2. Type your **store name** and **your name**, then press **Save**.
3. Set an **owner PIN** (4–6 digits). You will need it to void sales, change prices, allow utang over a limit, restore a backup or reset.
4. To practice first, press **Load demo data**. This fills the store with 30 days of sample sales. You can **Reset all data** later (type `BURAHIN` to confirm, or `DELETE` in English).

## Adding your paninda

1. Go to **Paninda (Products)** and press **Add product**.
2. Fill in the name, category, what one pack is called (*pack, bote, lata, strip…*), the **puhunan** (cost) and the **selling price**.
3. If you also sell it by piece (**tingi**), tick *Sold by piece too* and enter how many pieces are in a pack and the price per piece.
4. **Reorder level** is when you want to be reminded to buy more.
5. Selling prices must be payable with coins (multiples of 25 sentimo, e.g. ₱7.50 or ₱12.25).
6. If the price is lower than the puhunan, the system warns you. You can still save it, for example for an item that is about to expire.

Stock is shown as **"3 packs + 5 pcs"** for tingi items. To correct stock (spoiled, lost, used at home), press the **box** button on a product. Products that were already sold are **archived** instead of deleted, so old receipts stay correct.

Pictures are shown small. Click any product picture (in the product list, in the form or on the selling shelf) to see it large; close it with ✕, `Esc` or a click outside.

## 1. Bukas — opening the store

1. Count the money already in the drawer.
2. On **Bukas**, type it as the opening cash and press **Buksan ang tindahan**. The sign flips to **BUKAS**.

The Bukas screen then shows today's sales, kita, utang, the cash that should be in the drawer, items running low, and suki who are overdue.

## 2. Tinda — selling

1. Tap **+ pack** or **+ piece** under each product. Press `/` to search.
2. Check the **supot** (basket) on the right. Use **−/+** or type a quantity.
3. Choose the payment:
   - **Cash**: type what the customer gave, or tap **Exact / ₱100 / ₱500…**. The system shows the **sukli** and exactly which bills and coins to give.
   - **GCash**: type the 13-digit reference number.
   - **Utang**: choose the suki. If it is over their limit, you can allow it once with your PIN.
4. Press **BAYAD** (or `Enter`). The receipt appears, and you can **Print** it.

## 3. Listahan — utang

- **Add suki**: name, nickname, mobile number, and a credit limit (0 = no utang allowed).
- Click a suki to open their **notebook page**. Each utang and payment is written with the date and running balance. Paid lines are crossed out.
- To record a payment, enter the amount, choose Cash or GCash, and press **Save payment**.
- **Overdue** means the oldest unpaid utang is older than the number of days set in Ayos (default 15).

## 4. Benta — sales history

- Filter by date, payment type and status.
- Press **View** to see or reprint a receipt.
- **Void sale** (PIN + reason) cancels a mistake from today. The items go back to stock and any utang is reversed.

## 5. Pamilihan — restocking

1. Press **Add low-stock items**. The system suggests how many packs to buy based on the last 7 days of sales.
2. Add other items by hand.
3. Tick items as you buy them.
4. Back at the store, press **Received**, enter how many arrived and the new puhunan. The stock updates.
5. **Print** the list to bring to the palengke.

## 6. Gastos — taking cash out

Record any money taken from the drawer: *yelo*, plastic bags, pamasahe, money for the house, or payment to a supplier. These amounts are subtracted from the expected cash when closing.

## 7. Sara — closing the store

1. Count the bills and coins and type how many of each.
2. The system compares the count with the **expected cash** (opening + cash sales + utang paid in cash − gastos):
   - **TAMA**: exact.
   - **SOBRA**: there is extra money.
   - **KULANG**: money is missing. You must write a short note explaining why.
3. Press **Isara ang tindahan**. The day's summary appears and can be printed. After closing, that day can no longer be changed.

## Ulat — reports

Choose **Daily / Weekly / Monthly** to see sales, kita and expenses on a chart, plus the top sellers, products that are not selling, sales by category and by payment, and total utang outside. Use **Export** to download CSV files for Excel or Google Sheets.

## Talaan — activity log

Every important action is listed with the time: sales, voids, price changes, stock adjustments, payments, and opening and closing.

## Keeping your data safe

Data is saved **in this browser only**. Press **Ayos → Download backup** regularly and keep the file safe. To move to another computer, use **Restore backup** there.
